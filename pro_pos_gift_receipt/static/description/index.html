Odoo 18 Point of Sale Gift Receipt OWL Compatible

POS Gift Receipt (Qty Only)

A professional Odoo 18 Point of Sale extension that allows businesses to print a clean gift receipt containing only product names and quantities — without prices, taxes, discounts, or totals. Perfect for retail shops that want a polished gifting experience while preserving the standard POS flow.

Module Overview

POS Gift Receipt (Qty Only) adds a practical gifting feature to the Odoo POS workflow. After payment, the cashier can print a dedicated gift receipt for the customer. This receipt intentionally hides all financial values and keeps the output simple, clean, and gift-friendly.

Business Value: Improve customer experience in retail and gifting scenarios by offering a second receipt that protects pricing privacy while keeping the order details clear.

Key Features

POS Configuration Option

Enable or disable the Gift Receipt feature directly from POS settings.

Receipt Screen Integration

Adds a Gift Receipt button to the POS receipt screen after payment.

Qty-Only Output

Prints only product names and quantities without exposing any pricing details.

Safe POS Flow

Preserves the standard Odoo 18 POS receipt flow without breaking the original receipt process.

What the Gift Receipt Includes

  • Product Name
  • Quantity

What the Gift Receipt Excludes

  • Prices
  • Discounts
  • Taxes
  • Totals
  • Financial values of any kind

Use Cases

  • Retail stores selling products intended as gifts.
  • Shops that want to hide item prices from gift recipients.
  • Businesses that need a clean and professional secondary receipt after payment.
  • Stores looking to enhance customer satisfaction with a premium POS experience.
